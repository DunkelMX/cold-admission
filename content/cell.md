The cell was assembled for one operation and did not survive it intact.

What follows is the record its surviving operators keep for themselves: the filed
after-action reports, the dossiers of the agents assigned, and the questions nobody
has answered yet. It is maintained by the cell. There is no supervising authority,
no case officer signature, and no archive above this one.

One agent did not come back. One came back short an eye and without an extraction
record. Nobody has explained either.
