The cell had a ritual and no address. LEDGER found the address in the paperwork:
a shell company invoicing for resin volumes the plant's own output could not account
for, paid from accounts that closed and reopened under new names every eleven months.

He turned a paper anomaly into a building. He did not turn it into a person — the
ownership behind the shell is still open, and he has said plainly that the structure
was built by someone who expected exactly this kind of audit.
