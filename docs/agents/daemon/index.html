Background

Never on site. DAEMON works from wherever the building's systems can be reached, which in a plant built in the eighties and wired in the nineties is usually from anywhere at all.

On Gutter Key she was inside the facility's camera system before the cell reached the sewer grate, calling movement on the floor and cycling the door locks on the rear rooms as operators needed them. When the unit fragmented after insertion she was the only one who could still see all of it, and had no way to tell them so.
